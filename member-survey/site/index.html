How spots would be assigned

Proposed only, for discussion. Nothing here is final.

  1. Answers go into one spreadsheet, one row per member.
  2. Members playing in our home invitational are placed first.
  3. Each club's spots are filled from 1st choices, then 2nd, then 3rd. Ties within a group are drawn at random.
  4. Anyone still unplaced is offered an open club within her drive time, if she said yes to an alternate.
  5. Friends can swap spots after the draw, with the committee's OK.
