A trusted operator for the clients you already serve.

Estate attorneys, wealth managers, private bankers and family office leaders are often the first to see when a principal's household has outgrown the way it is run. I work alongside you, never around you, and take on the operational layer your clients need but your firm is not built to provide.

Working Together

How I work with advisors

I.

Clear lines.

I manage the household and its operations. Legal, investment, tax and fiduciary matters remain entirely with you.

II.

Prepared, not noisy.

Documents, invoices, renewals and decisions reach you organised and ready for review, on your preferred cadence.

III.

Continuity.

Properties, staff, vendors and obligations are fully documented, so a household is ready for transitions, whether seasonal, generational or in a change of staff.

IV.

Discretion.

Standing confidentiality on every engagement. A mutual confidentiality agreement is available before any detailed conversation.
