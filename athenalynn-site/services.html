Case Study

Selected engagement

Private Chief of Staff to the owner of a multi-location specialty practice

Georgia · Remote · Ongoing

The situation. The owner of a growing two-office specialty practice was carrying the business, the household and his travel personally, with information spread across calendars, inboxes and spreadsheets.

The engagement. I became the single operating point across the practice, the finances, the household and travel: hiring and staff coordination, schedule oversight across both offices, continuing-education compliance, contract review and facility projects; routing expenses and approvals for the owner's decision and preparing items for the practice CFO; managing residence services, vendors, vehicles and insurance renewals; and planning domestic and international travel.

The system. Everything runs through a private executive dashboard I designed and built for him, with return-date tracking on every open item, so nothing depends on memory and nothing reaches the owner before it is ready for a decision.

Onboarding

The first ninety days

Every engagement begins the same way: by understanding the household as it actually runs, then building the structure it should run on.

I.

Understand

Weeks one to three

A private operational review of residences, staff, vendors, obligations, travel patterns and existing advisors. Nothing changes yet. Everything is documented.

II.

Build

Weeks four to eight

The household's private system is configured and populated: properties, staff, vendors, finances, calendars and obligations, each with an owner and a return date. Standards and handoffs are written down.

III.

Run

Week nine onward

Steady-state management. The principal receives a regular briefing at the cadence they prefer, and decisions arrive prepared. Scope is reviewed together each quarter.
