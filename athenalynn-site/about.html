Origins

I was born and raised in New York — shaped by the pace, precision, and standards of a city that demands both. My career began on the agency floors of Manhattan, where I spent two decades managing global campaigns and leading teams at Ogilvy & Mather, McCann, Digitas, and DraftFCB. The work was complex, the clients were demanding, and the margin for error was none. I thrived in it.

The move to Palm Beach was intentional. My husband John and I had been coming south for years before we made it permanent — drawn by the quality of life, the community, and the particular kind of excellence this place quietly expects.

I am engaged here — genuinely so. I support animal welfare causes, attend charitable events, and believe that a life well lived includes giving back to the community around you. When I am not working, I am on the golf course, at the table with friends, or finding the next reason to be glad we stayed.

The pivot from agency life to private operations was not a departure — it was a refinement. The same instincts that built campaigns for global brands now manage the complexity of private lives and portfolios. The standards did not change. Only the scale became more personal.

Background

My work sits at the intersection of estate operations, strategic administration, and principal-level support. I have built and managed comprehensive operational infrastructure for private households and complex portfolios across Palm Beach and beyond. In practice, that means serving as estate manager, household manager and private chief of staff, and building the private systems a household runs on.

What I offer is not a service — it is a retained presence. I become an integrated part of the principal's infrastructure, operating with the discretion of a trusted inner-circle presence and the precision of a seasoned operator.

Principals come to me when operational friction is no longer acceptable. When continuity, standards, and proactive oversight are not optional. When they need one person who sees the whole board — and acts accordingly.
