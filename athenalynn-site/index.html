Engagement Model

How I work.

I do not work transactionally. Engagement is long-term, integrated, and structured around the principal's priorities — not a menu of services.

I.

Private Retainer

All engagements begin with a private introduction and are structured as retained relationships. I do not take on projects. I integrate.

II.

Singular Focus

My capacity is intentionally limited. Each principal receives complete attention. There is no dilution, no junior staff, no delegation of judgment.

III.

Whole-Board Perspective

I see across the full scope — assets, obligations, relationships, and timelines. Continuity and coherence are maintained from the inside.

IV.

Strict Confidentiality

All engagements are conducted under strict, standing confidentiality. Discretion is not a feature — it is the foundation of everything I do.
